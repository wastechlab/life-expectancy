# Code of Conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews or discussions, agrees to the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It asks
for respect and patience with people whose views and experience differ from yours, and it rules out harassment,
insults and personal attacks of any kind.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here troubles you, tell the maintainer privately, by the email on the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/VladimirMakarevich). Every report is read, kept confidential and answered.

## Enforcement

The maintainer acts on reports following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a private word up to a
permanent ban, depending on what happened.
